=== Main Tables ===

Table 1 | Immune Cell Proportions Across Disease Groups in Liver and Blood | Donor-level means (%). Mann-Whitney U tests (two-sided) vs NL; ★p<0.05. Liver: NL n=6, IT n=6, IA n=5, AR n=3, CR n=3. Blood: NL n=5, IT n=5, IA n=4, AR n=3, CR n=3. The chronic HBV disease stages (IT→IA→CR) represent sequential phases of vertical infection. AR is an independent comparator of adult-onset acute self-limited hepatitis B.

Table 2 | IT-Specific Individual Gene Markers (Top Candidates by Tissue) | C5 148-gene panel (C3-only genes noted where applicable). Donor-level Mann-Whitney U tests. Percent change computed from donor-level mean log-normalized expression values (log1p-transformed counts per 10,000). IT-specific: NL→IT significant (p<0.05) with NL→IA non-significant (p≥0.05). Consistency: donor pairs showing expected direction / total possible pairs.

Table 3 | BCR/TCR Donor-Level Repertoire Metrics Across the HBV Disease Spectrum | Panel A: BCR repertoire metrics. Panel B: TCR repertoire metrics. Values: donor-level means ± SD. Clonality = 1 − (Shannon entropy / log2(n_unique_clones)). Singleton% = percentage of clones with single cell. Class-switched% = IgG% + IgA%. BCR: heavy chain (IGH) only; TCR: alpha chain (TRA) only. CR group had no BCR/TCR library data. AR (n=1 per tissue) shown for reference, excluded from statistical testing. Statistical comparisons reported in text (Result 5) and Supplementary Table S12.
