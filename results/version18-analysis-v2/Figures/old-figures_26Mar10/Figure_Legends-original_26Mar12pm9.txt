Figure 1. Tissue-Separated Immune Cell Composition Across the HBV Disease Spectrum. (A) Stacked bar plots showing immune cell lineage proportions in liver across NL, IT, IA, AR, and CR groups (donor-level means, 6 lineages). (B) Same for blood. Liver is dominated by NK cells (43.9% at NL) while blood is dominated by myeloid cells (35.1% at NL). (C) NK cell proportions shown separately for liver (red circles) and blood (blue triangles). Individual donor values with group means (horizontal bars). Liver NK shows progressive decline with partial CR recovery; blood NK shows severe CR depletion. Mann-Whitney U test; ★p<0.05. Panels: 3 (A, B, C)

Figure 2. Pathway-Level Tissue Discordance in IT Phase. (A) Heatmap of 29 pathway AUCell scores (rows) across 6 lineages (columns) showing NL→IT percent change in liver. Red: upregulation; blue: downregulation. ★p<0.05, †p<0.10. (B) Same for blood. Gold borders: 9 tissue-opposite pathway-lineage combinations (Table 2) showing significant change in one tissue with opposite direction in the other. Panels: 2 (A, B)

Figure 3. IT Gene Signatures Significant in Both Liver and Blood. (A) HLA-DPB1 donor-level dot plots across disease groups in myeloid cells, shown for liver (red circles) and blood (blue triangles). (B) DNMT1 myeloid donor-level dot plots. (C) JAK1 cross-lineage NL→IT percent change in liver and blood, showing pan-lineage elevation. ★p<0.05. (D) PRDM1 downregulation in CD4+ T cells in both liver and blood. Panels: 4 (A–D)

Figure 4. IT-Specific Gene Signatures by Tissue Compartment. (A) TOX in CD4+ T cells showing liver-specific upregulation (★p=0.009) with blood non-significant (tissue-opposite). (B) LAYN in CD4+ T cells, same liver-specific pattern (★p=0.009). (C) SOCS1 in CD4+ T cells showing blood-specific IT downregulation (★p=0.008; JAK-STAT brake release). (D) TYROBP in plasma B cells showing cytotoxic phenotype acquisition in both liver (★p=0.009) and blood (★p=0.008). Panels: 4 (A–D)

Figure 5. Gene-to-Gene Correlation Networks Reveal Coordinated Immune Reprogramming in IT. (A) Gene correlation network constructed from donor-level Spearman correlations (FDR q<0.05, |ρ|≥0.7, strongest per gene pair across all tissue-lineage combinations). Nodes represent the 15 hub genes (Table 4) plus TET2 and SOCS1. Node colors indicate functional modules: Mitochondrial (cyan; MT-CYB, MT-ND1, MT-ND2, TFAM, SDHB), Signaling (orange; JAK1, TGFBR2, MTOR, RPTOR), Epigenetic (purple; DNMT1, DNMT3A, TET2), and Other Hub Genes (grey; CD44, CD27, ATM, RB1). SOCS1 (amber) is included to illustrate the JAK-STAT paradox. Green solid lines indicate positive correlations; red dashed lines indicate inverse correlations. Edge thickness is proportional to |ρ|. Key ρ values are annotated. (B) TFAM↔DNMT1 correlation in Blood Myeloid cells (ρ=0.925, p<0.001), demonstrating mito-epigenetic coupling. (C) JAK1↔SOCS1 inverse correlation in Blood CD4+ T cells (ρ=−0.731, p=0.0003), illustrating the JAK-STAT paradox. In B and C, each dot represents one donor, colored by disease group (NL, green; IT, red; IA, orange; AR, blue; CR, purple).

Figure 6. Disease Trajectory: IT→IA Transition and CR Scar. (A) TGFB1 in blood NK cells across disease spectrum, showing IA-emergent upregulation (NL→IT NS; IT→IA ★↑43.9%, p=0.016, 20/20 consistency). TGFB1 elevation in blood NK becomes significant only upon transition to IA, indicating an IA-emergent immunoregulatory phenotype rather than continuation of an IT-specific program. (B) GNLY in CD8+ T cells, showing preserved cytotoxicity in AR versus diminished in IA. (C) MEFV in CD8+ T cells, showing aberrant inflammasome expression unique to CR (immunologic scar). Individual donor values (dots) with group means (horizontal bars). Mann-Whitney U test; ★p<0.05. Panels: 3 (A–C)

Figure 7. Six-Layer Effector Suppression Architecture in IT Phase. Schematic model illustrating six concurrent transcriptomic patterns preventing effector function despite active immune engagement. Blood-dominant layers (L1-L4): myeloid paracrine suppression (TGFB1/LGALS9), epigenetic silencing (DNMT1/DNMT3A/TET2), metabolic checkpoint (MTOR↑/LDHA↓), JAK-STAT paradox (SOCS1/3↓). Liver-dominant layer (L5): T cell exhaustion (TOX/LAYN). Both-tissue layer (L6): terminal differentiation block (PRDM1↓). [Schematic — generated separately] Panel: 1
